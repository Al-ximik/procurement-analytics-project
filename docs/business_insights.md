# Business Insights

## Executive summary

The synthetic procurement dataset contains 10,000 transactions with total actual spend of **611.09M AZN**, estimated spend of **643.17M AZN**, and net savings of **34.97M AZN**.

## Savings and procurement methods

- Overall savings rate: **5.44%**.
- Open Tender generated the strongest net savings: **23.82M AZN** and **8.65%** savings.
- Direct Purchase generated **-1.15M AZN** net savings, indicating overall overspend against estimate.
- 7,969 transactions generated positive savings, while 1,952 generated overspend; 11 were approximately neutral.

## Supplier performance and risk

- Supplier performance is evaluated using quality and delivery metrics.
- The segmentation benchmarks are **50% on-time delivery** and **4.0/5 quality**.
- Critical Supplier + Delivery Risk segments represent **54.15%** of analyzed supplier spend.
- High-risk supplier spend totals approximately **109.88M AZN**.
- Transport alone represents **80.82%** of high-risk supplier spend.

## Monthly spend volatility

Largest month-over-month spend increases include:

1. 2025-03: **+57.07%**
2. 2024-08: **+38.17%**
3. 2024-03: **+36.26%**
4. 2024-12: **+34.27%**
5. 2025-10: **+34.03%**

Largest decreases include:

1. 2024-09: **-32.59%**
2. 2025-02: **-30.73%**
3. 2025-01: **-30.30%**
4. 2026-03: **-25.35%**
5. 2024-04: **-21.95%**

## Data quality and analytical limitations

- 59 missing supplier IDs
- 68 missing estimated unit prices
- 21 completed procurements missing actual delivery dates
- 28 completed procurements missing quality scores
- The department budget field is not on the same apparent scale as procurement spend and is treated as an unresolved business-definition issue rather than a valid overspend KPI.
